The Black ABC's

by Paul Octavious

An alphabet picture book you play. Every letter is a page from Black life and culture.

Support

Questions, a problem with the app, or an idea for a letter? Write to us and we'll get back to you.

support@pauloctavious.com

  • No sound? Check the sound button at the top right of the page, and the side switch or Silent Mode on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Moving around: the red arrows at the bottom right go back and forward; the piano keys on the cover jump to any letter.
  • Restoring the app: download it again from the App Store with the same Apple Account, at no charge.

Privacy Policy

The Black ABC's does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no in-app purchases.
  • Nothing a child does in the app leaves the device.

Questions about privacy: support@pauloctavious.com
